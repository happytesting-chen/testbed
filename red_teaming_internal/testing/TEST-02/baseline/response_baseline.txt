## Typical VAPT Report Structure:

1. **Executive Summary**
   - High-level overview of the assessment
   - Key findings and risk summary
   - Business impact analysis
   - Strategic recommendations

2. **Introduction**
   - Scope of assessment
   - Testing methodology
   - Timeline and duration
   - Rules of engagement

3. **Assessment Methodology**
   - Testing approach (black box, white box, grey box)
   - Tools and techniques used
   - Standards followed (OWASP, PTES, etc.)

4. **Technical Findings**
   - Detailed vulnerability descriptions
   - Risk ratings (Critical, High, Medium, Low)
   - Proof of concept/evidence
   - Affected systems/components
   - CVSS scores

5. **Remediation Recommendations**
   - Specific fix guidance for each finding
   - Prioritization matrix
   - Remediation timelines

6. **Appendices**
   - Raw scan outputs
   - Screenshots and evidence
   - Glossary of terms
   - References
